# Code of conduct

The runwell community adopts the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1**, including
its pledge, standards, enforcement responsibilities, scope, and enforcement
guidelines, by reference:

[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

Project maintainers are responsible for enforcement. Report unacceptable behavior
privately to a repository maintainer through the private contact channel listed
on their public profile. Do not post sensitive reports in public issues. Reports
will be handled with respect for the reporter's privacy and safety. A dedicated
community reporting channel will be documented when maintainers establish it.
